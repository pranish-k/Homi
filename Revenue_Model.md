# Homi Revenue Model & Financial Projections

## Executive Summary
Multi-stream revenue model targeting both B2C (individual users) and B2B (institutions) markets with freemium and enterprise licensing strategies.

---

## Revenue Streams

### 1. B2C - Individual Users (Freemium Model)

#### Pricing Tiers
- **Free Tier**: 1-4 users per group
  - 10 receipt uploads/month
  - Basic expense splitting
  - Standard payment integration

- **Premium Tier**: $9.99/month per group
  - 5-15 users per group
  - Unlimited receipt uploads
  - Advanced analytics & insights
  - Priority support
  - Export reports
  - Custom categories

- **Premium Plus**: $19.99/month per group
  - 15+ users (large houses/shared living)
  - All Premium features
  - Auto-recurring bill splits
  - Receipt OCR with auto-categorization
  - Tax documentation export

#### Annual Plans (17% discount)
- Premium Annual: $99/year (save $20)
- Premium Plus Annual: $199/year (save $40)

---

### 2. B2C - Transaction Fees

#### Payment Processing Revenue Share
- **Small fee per transaction**: $0.25 per payment processed
- **Alternative**: 1.5% of transaction value (capped at $2.00)
  - *Note: Must verify feasibility with Stripe/PayPal TOS*

#### Premium Transaction Features
- **Instant Settlement**: $0.99 per instant debt clearing
- **Payment Protection**: $1.99 per protected transaction (dispute insurance)

---

### 3. B2B - College/University Licensing

#### Institutional Pricing (Per Student/Year)
- **Tier 1** (500-1,500 students): $3.00/student/year
- **Tier 2** (1,501-5,000 students): $2.50/student/year
- **Tier 3** (5,001-15,000 students): $2.00/student/year
- **Tier 4** (15,001+ students): $1.50/student/year

#### Enhanced Features for Institutions
- Bulk onboarding & group creation
- RA/Housing admin dashboard
- Custom roommate agreements
- Broadcast messaging
- Dorm inventory management
- Integration with housing systems
- Compliance & reporting tools

#### Implementation Fee
- One-time setup: $5,000-$15,000 (based on student count)

---

### 4. B2B - Property Management

#### Per-Property Pricing
- **Small Properties** (10-50 units): $99/month
- **Medium Properties** (51-200 units): $299/month
- **Large Properties** (201-500 units): $599/month
- **Enterprise** (500+ units): Custom pricing

#### Add-on Modules
- Maintenance request system: +$49/month
- Lease management: +$99/month
- Tenant screening integration: +$79/month

---

### 5. Affiliate & Partnership Revenue

#### Potential Partners
- **Renters Insurance**: $20-40 commission per signup
- **Furniture/Moving Services**: 3-5% of purchase
- **Utilities Setup Services**: $15-25 per activation
- **Credit Building Services**: $30-50 per enrollment

#### Conservative Estimate
- 5% of active users engage monthly
- Average commission: $25
- Per 1,000 active users = $1,250/month

### 6. Data & Insights (Future Revenue Stream)

#### Anonymized Market Research
- Aggregated spending trends
- Housing preference data
- Student/renter behavior insights
- Estimated: $0.50-1.00 per active user annually (sold to research firms)

- **Lifetime Value (LTV)**:

  - LTV:CAC Ratio: 5.6-9.3x ✓ (Healthy: >3x)

- **College Partnership LTV**:

## Risk Mitigation

### Revenue Risks
1. **Low conversion rate**: Minimum viable - 2% conversion still profitable by Year 3
2. **College sales cycles**: Long (6-12 months) - need cash runway
3. **Competition**: Splitwise, Venmo - differentiate with institutional features
4. **Payment processor restrictions**: Have fallback pricing models

### Mitigation Strategies
- Diversified revenue streams (not dependent on single source)
- Focus on sticky institutional contracts
- Build network effects (groups invite groups)
- Create switching costs (historical data, integrations)
